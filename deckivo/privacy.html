Deckivo Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2026

Deckivo (“the app”) is a party word game developed by Henry Harvey. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Summary

  • Deckivo does not require an account.
  • Deckivo does not sell your personal information or use third-party advertising analytics.
  • Most data stays on your device (decks, game history, settings, in-progress games).
  • If you turn on Wiki Lookup, the app may send the current card word to Wikipedia to fetch a short hint.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your phone or browser:

  • Custom decks you create
  • Completed game history and scores
  • In-progress game drafts (so you can resume a game)
  • App preferences (theme color and Wiki Lookup on/off)

This data is saved using on-device storage (for example AsyncStorage on mobile). It is not uploaded to our servers because Deckivo does not operate a backend service for gameplay data.

Network use (optional Wiki Lookup)

Wiki Lookup is off by default and can be enabled in Settings. When enabled during a turn, the app may send requests to Wikipedia to look up the word shown on the current card:

  • Wikipedia REST API (en.wikipedia.org)
  • Wikipedia Search API (used as a fallback when a direct summary is not found)

Those requests are sent to Wikimedia Foundation services, not to us. What Wikipedia receives is typically the search term (the card word). We do not add your name, account ID, or other personal profile information to those requests because the app has no accounts.

Wikipedia’s handling of requests is governed by Wikimedia’s privacy policy.

Information we do not collect

Deckivo does not intentionally collect:

  • Name, email address, or login credentials
  • Precise location
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data
  • Payment details (purchases are processed by Apple or Google)

Children

Deckivo is intended for general audiences. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help address the concern.

Data retention and deletion

Data on your device remains until you delete it or remove the app. You can delete custom decks and game history inside the app. Uninstalling Deckivo removes locally stored app data from your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will be revised when changes are posted to this page.
